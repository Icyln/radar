# Radar User Guide

## 1. Create a Job Alert

Open **Job Alerts** and create a focused search. Add the role names you actually want, preferred locations, work style, and how recent a job should be.

Radar searches broadly by default. Under **More options**, choose **Only search companies I follow** only when you intentionally want a company-restricted alert.

Keep alerts focused. A few specific titles generally produce better results than many loosely related titles.

## 2. Review Jobs

The Jobs page is organized as:

- **For You** — jobs matching active alerts
- **All Jobs** — jobs Radar has found that you can browse
- **Saved** — jobs you marked for later
- **Hidden** — jobs you chose not to see in the normal list

Basic filters cover keyword, work style, and posting age. Technical source filters are kept under **More filters**.

## 3. Follow Companies

Open **Companies** to search Radar's monitored company list and follow employers you care about.

If a company is missing, use **Request a company** and paste its public careers URL. Radar will check the source in a bounded background scan. You do not need to know the company's ATS provider or identifier.

## 4. Connect Telegram

Open **Settings** and choose **Connect Telegram**. Complete the link in Telegram, then return to Radar.

Use **Send test** to confirm delivery. Once connected, Radar can send new matching jobs to your chat. The jobs remain available in the workspace even if Telegram is disconnected.

## 5. Choose light or dark workspace mode

Settings includes a light/dark switch for the authenticated workspace. The public landing and help pages intentionally use Radar's fixed public theme.

## Recommended setup

Start with one or two Job Alerts for your strongest target roles, keep broad searching enabled, and connect Telegram. Add followed companies only where you have a specific employer preference.
